🤖 How Does It Work?

Detection Algorithm

Our AI detection engine analyzes your text across multiple dimensions:

  • Transitional Phrases (30%): Detects formal phrases like "furthermore", "in conclusion"
  • Sentence Consistency (25%): Checks for unnaturally similar sentence lengths
  • Word Repetition (15%): Identifies overused words and phrases
  • Formal Punctuation (20%): Measures semicolons and colons usage
  • Vocabulary Variety (10%): Evaluates the uniqueness of word choices

Humanization Process

Once AI patterns are detected, our humanization engine:

  • Replaces formal transitions with casual, natural alternatives
  • Breaks up long, structured sentences
  • Adds subtle human touches like conversational interjections
  • Varies sentence structure and rhythm
  • Maintains the original meaning and information
